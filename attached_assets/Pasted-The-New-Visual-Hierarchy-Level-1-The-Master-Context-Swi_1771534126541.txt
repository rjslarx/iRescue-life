The New Visual Hierarchy
Level 1: The Master Context Switch (Top)
A large, prominent Segmented Control or Toggle: [ 🏢 In Shelter ] | [ 🏠 In Foster ]

Level 2: The Contextual Navigation (Middle)
The tabs that appear dynamically based on the toggle selected in Level 1.

Level 3: The Data (Bottom)
The summary cards and the data tables that belong to that specific tab and location.

Tab Mapping: What to Show and Hide
When you flip that master switch, the navigation tabs should change to match the operational reality of that location.

When [ 🏢 In Shelter ] is selected:
You are putting on your "Shelter Meds" hat. Show these tabs:

Intake Protocol (Intakes almost always happen at the physical facility).

Surgery Queue (Shelter dogs needing spay/neuter).

Active Treatments (The digital med-cart for kennel techs).

Preventative Care (Routine shelter deworming/vaccines).

HIDDEN: Vet Visit Triage.

When [ 🏠 In Foster ] is selected:
You are putting on your "Volunteer Logistics" hat. Show these tabs:

Vet Visit Triage (Front and center, because this is where fosters upload their invoices and questions).

Active Treatments (Tracking foster compliance for daily meds).

Surgery Queue (Scheduling foster dogs to come back in for their spay/neuter).

Preventative Care (Tracking which fosters need to pick up more Heartgard).

HIDDEN: Intake Protocol.

3. Prop Drilling / Context Provider:
