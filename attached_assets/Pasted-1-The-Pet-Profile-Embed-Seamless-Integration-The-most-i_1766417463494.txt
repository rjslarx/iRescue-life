1. The "Pet Profile" Embed (Seamless Integration)
The most immediate way to help your tenants is to allow them to "link" a specific Zeffy donation form to each dog profile on your platform.

The Idea: On the iRescue.life dashboard, give rescues a field to paste their Zeffy Embed Code.

The Benefit: On the public dog profile page, visitors will see a beautiful, branded "Sponsor [Dog Name]" or "Pay Adoption Fee" button. When clicked, it opens a Zeffy checkout overlay right on your site, meaning the donor never has to leave iRescue.life.

Implementation: Use an <iframe> or Zeffy’s provided JavaScript snippet within your Replit code to render the form dynamically based on the tenant's ID.

2. Automation via Zapier (The "SaaS" Power Move)
Zeffy doesn't have a fully "open" public API for developers like Stripe does, but they have a very robust Zapier Integration.

The Idea: Use Zapier as the "bridge" between Zeffy and your iRescue.life database.

The Workflow: 1. A donor pays an adoption fee on Zeffy. 2. Zeffy triggers a "New Donation" event in Zapier. 3. Zapier sends a Webhook to your Replit backend. 4. Your backend automatically updates that dog’s status from "Available" to "Adopted" and logs the donor’s info.

3. "Sponsor-a-Pet" Recurring Programs
Rescues struggle with "feast or famine" funding. You can use Zeffy’s recurring gift feature to create a Sustainer Program for your tenants.

The Feature: Enable a "Monthly Guardian" button on the rescue’s landing page.

Why it works: Zeffy handles the monthly credit card billing, the "expired card" emails, and the end-of-year tax receipts automatically. Your platform simply displays the "Total Monthly Support" progress bar to show how much the rescue has raised.

4. Tap-to-Pay for In-Person Events
One of Zeffy’s newest and best features for rescues is Tap to Pay on iPhone/Android.

The Idea: Encourage your tenants to use the Zeffy app at adoption events.

The Benefit: Volunteers can accept "Credit Card" donations just by having the donor tap their phone or card against the volunteer's phone. No hardware (like a Square reader) is required, and there are still zero fees.

iRescue.life Integration: You could provide a "QR Code Generator" in your dashboard that links directly to the rescue's Zeffy Tap-to-Pay link for easy scanning at events.

5. Automated Tax Receipts
For a medium-sized rescue, writing tax receipts is a chore that usually happens in a panicked rush in January.

The Integration: By routing all payments through Zeffy, your tenants can honestly tell their donors: "You will receive an IRS-compliant tax receipt via email the second you hit 'Submit'." * Your Value-Add: You can tell your tenants that iRescue.life "takes the paperwork off your plate" by leveraging this Zeffy feature.
