To handle Feedback, you need to pair the Compliance Widget with a second component: The "Action Center" (or Triage Feed).

Here is how to design the Tenant Dashboard to handle both Compliance (Missing Data) and Feedback (Incoming Data).

1. The Dashboard Layout: "Silence vs. Noise"
Imagine the Tenant's dashboard split into two distinct columns or zones.

Left Column: The Compliance Monitor (The "Silence")
Goal: Catch people who are ignoring you.

Logic: "If due_date < now AND status != done".

What it shows:

🔴 Overdue Meds: "3 Fosters missed heartworm prevention yesterday."

🔴 Missing Content: "Spot (Foster: Sarah) has been in care 7 days but has no Bio."

🔴 Ghosting: "Adopter Mike hasn't opened the '6-month Check-in'."

Right Column: The Action Feed (The "Noise")
Goal: Process incoming requests and updates.

Logic: "New row inserted in feedback, supply_requests, or medical_logs".

What it shows:

🟢 Foster Update: "Sarah logged: 'Spot ate well, but stool is loose.'" (Medical)

📦 Supply Request: "Mike needs: Puppy Pads." (Inventory)

💌 Happy Tail: "Adopter Jen uploaded a photo: 'First nap on the couch!'" (Marketing)

2. Handling Foster Feedback (Operational)
Fosters generate work for the rescue. Their feedback needs to be routed to specific departments (Medical vs. Supplies).

The "Red Flag" Logic: You don't want the Tenant to read every single "He ate dinner" log. You need to filter for Exceptions.

Standard Log: "Ate 1 cup. Activity Normal." -> System Action: Save to DB, do not notify Admin.

Alert Log: "Vomiting. Lethargic." -> System Action: Trigger "Medical Alert" in the Action Feed. Highlight in RED.

The Supply Workflow:

Foster Input: Clicks "Request Supplies" -> Selects "Heartworm Prevention" -> Quantity: 1.

Tenant View: Appears in the Action Feed.

Action Buttons: [Mark Ready for Pickup] | [Out of Stock].

Closing the Loop: When the Tenant clicks [Mark Ready], the Foster gets a Push Notification: "Your meds are at the front desk!"

3. Handling Adopter Feedback (Marketing & Retention)
Adopter feedback is valuable content. You need to make it easy to re-purpose it.

The "Happy Tail" Pipeline:

Adopter Input: Responds to the "3-Day Check-in" with a photo of the dog.

Tenant View: Appears in the Action Feed as "Marketing Gold."

Action Button: [Post to Facebook] (or [Download for Socials]).

Action Button: [Save to 'Success Stories' Page].

Why this matters: Most rescues lose these photos in random text threads. Your platform centralizes them into a content library.

The "Issue" Triage:

Adopter Input: Responds to a survey: "He is growling at my husband."

Tenant View: This creates a "Behavior Ticket."

It shouldn't just sit in a feed. It should trigger an email to the rescue's Trainer or Director immediately.

4. How to Build the "Action Center" (UI Concept)
Don't build a complex ticketing system (like Jira). Just build a "Done" button feed.

Visual Concept: A list of cards sorted by "Newest." Each card must have a way to dismiss it.

[ MEDICAL ALERT ] - 2 mins ago Foster: Sarah (Dog: Spot) Note: "Spot has a rash on his belly." [View Photo]

[Reply] [Assign to Vet] [Dismiss]

[ SUPPLY REQUEST ] - 1 hour ago Foster: Mike Item: Large Crate

[Approve] [Deny]

[ ADOPTER UPDATE ] - 3 hours ago Adopter: Emily (Dog: Luna) Note: "Luna loves the cat!"

[Add to Bio] [Share] [Dismiss]

5. Summary: The Complete "Control Room"
To fully monitor your tenants' operations, your Dashboard needs three widgets, not one:

Compliance (The Watchdog): Who missed a deadline? (Automated Nudges).

Action Center (The Inbox): Who needs help or sent data? (Triage).

Inventory (The Warehouse): Who needs supplies? (Logistics).
