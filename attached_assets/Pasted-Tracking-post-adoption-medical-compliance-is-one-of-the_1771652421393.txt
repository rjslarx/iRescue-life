Tracking post-adoption medical compliance is one of the most critical legal and operational functions of a rescue. If an animal is adopted out before it is fully vetted, the rescue needs a bulletproof system to ensure the adopter fulfills their end of the bargain.
Here is the breakdown of what your "Adoption Compliance Monitor" should track, and the exact workflow you should build for the checkout process.
Part 1: What to Monitor in the Compliance Dashboard
Your compliance monitor should focus exclusively on high-stakes, legally binding, or life-saving procedures. You don't need to monitor if the adopter gave a monthly flea pill, but you do need to monitor these four categories:
 * Spay/Neuter (Alteration): This is the most common compliance metric tracked by shelters. Adoption contracts frequently stipulate that unaltered animals must be fixed by a specific age. This is often tied to a refundable spay/neuter deposit or a voucher system.
 * Rabies Vaccination: Because Rabies is a public health issue and a legal requirement for licensing, rescues must ensure puppies and kittens receive this once they are of age.
 * Puppy/Kitten Booster Series: If an 8-week-old puppy is adopted, they still need their 12-week and 16-week DHPP boosters to survive Parvo.
 * Ongoing Medical Treatments: If a dog is adopted while recovering from an injury or illness, the monitor should track the arrangement for follow-up care or the completion of prescribed treatments (like antibiotics or heartworm protocols).
Part 2: The Workflow (Carry Over vs. Confirm)
The Verdict: You must use a Hybrid Approach (Auto-Populate + Human Verification). You should never allow the dates to carry over from the medical record directly into the compliance monitor without a confirmation step during checkout.
Here is why a mandatory confirmation step is the "Gold Standard":
 * Medical Dates vs. Legal Dates: The animal's medical record holds the clinical due date, but the adoption checkout creates the legal due date.
 * Private Vet Policies: Your rescue's medical record might state a puppy is due for a spay at 4 months old. However, the adopter's private vet might refuse to spay the dog until it has its first heat, or until it is 1 year old. The rescue staff needs the ability to override the standard date during checkout to honor the adopter's vet timeline, extending the date on the contract.
 * Relative Timelines: Sometimes, a compliance date isn't tied to the animal's age, but to the adoption date itself. For example, the staff may need to manually select a date on a calendar to indicate when the adopter must return to the shelter for a required surgery or to provide proof of completion.
If you force the staff to start with a blank slate, it wastes time. If you blind-copy the medical records, you end up with contracts that have impossible or legally inaccurate deadlines.
How to Build the "Compliance Review" Checkout Step
To make this frictionless for your rescue tenants, you want the system to do the heavy lifting but give the human the final say.
Copy/Paste this prompt into Replit Agent:
I need to add a "Compliance Review" step to the Adoption Checkout flow.

1. The Trigger:
   - When a user initiates the "Adopt Animal" workflow, the system must check the animal's `medical_records` and `preventative_care` tables.
   - Look for any tasks marked `status: 'pending'` that are flagged as `is_compliance_required: true` (e.g., Spay/Neuter, Rabies, Heartworm Treatment).

2. The UI (The Review Modal):
   - Before generating the adoption contract, display a modal: "Pending Medical Compliance."
   - For each pending item, display a row with:
     - The Procedure Name (Read-only).
     - The "Suggested Due Date" (Auto-populated from the medical record).
     - An editable Date Picker field: "Contractual Due Date."
     - A checkbox: "Require Deposit / Hold?"

3. The Logic:
   - The user can accept the auto-populated date OR change it (e.g., extending a spay date to accommodate a private vet).
   - When the user clicks "Confirm & Generate Contract":
     - The selected dates are injected into the PDF Adoption Contract.
     - The tasks are moved to the `compliance_monitor` table, linked to the new Adopter's profile, using the new Contractual Due Date.

By building it this way, the rescue staff saves time because the dates are pre-filled, but they retain the legal authority to adjust the terms of the contract before the adopter signs it.
