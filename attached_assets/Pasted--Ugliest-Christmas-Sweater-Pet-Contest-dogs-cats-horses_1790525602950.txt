* Ugliest Christmas Sweater Pet Contest — dogs, cats, horses, anything in festive attire. Great for December.
* Santa Paws Portrait Contest — cutest Christmas photo, with the winning pet featured on a holiday card or social-media banner.
* Valentine’s “Love Your Pet” Contest — cutest couple, best pet-and-owner photo, sweetest rescue story, or “most lovable face.”
* Mardi Gras Pets — this could be particularly fun in Louisiana: costumes, beads, purple/gold/green, “King or Queen of Haseya.”
* Springtime / Easter Pet Portraits — bunny ears, flowers, pastel backgrounds, “Best Easter Bonnet.”
* Patriotic Pets — red, white and blue portraits around July 4.
* Beach / Summer Pets — sunglasses, pools, Hawaiian shirts, “Coolest Pet of Summer.”
* Back-to-School Pets — glasses, backpacks, “Class Clown,” “Most Likely to Steal Your Lunch.”
* Halloween Costume Contest — probably one of the strongest, especially if you divide it into funniest, cutest, and most creative.
* Pet Look-Alike Contest — “Does your dog look like you?” Owner and pet photos can be hilarious and highly shareable.
* Best Pet Smile — extremely easy for people to enter and works year-round.
* Best Head Tilt — tailor-made for dogs and naturally funny.
* Funniest Sleeping Position — another low-effort contest with lots of good entries.
* Most Dramatic Pet — side-eye, guilty looks, tantrums, “I haven’t eaten in 10 minutes” expressions.
* Best Rescue Glow-Up — before-and-after adoption photos. This one also reinforces the rescue mission beautifully.
* Senior Pet Superstar — celebrates older pets and could tie into senior animal adoption awareness.
* Special Needs Superstars — thoughtfully celebrates pets thriving despite disabilities or medical challenges.
* Rescue Alumni Calendar Contest — winners become the 12 monthly pets in a Haseya calendar. This could potentially be one of your strongest annual fundraisers.
* Pet of the Year — one larger annual contest, perhaps with monthly preliminary winners competing for the final title.
