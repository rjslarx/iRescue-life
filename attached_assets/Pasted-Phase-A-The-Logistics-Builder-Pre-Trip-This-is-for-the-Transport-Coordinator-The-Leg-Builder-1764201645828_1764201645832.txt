Phase A: The Logistics Builder (Pre-Trip)
This is for the Transport Coordinator.
• The "Leg" Builder: Ability to define stops.
• Leg 1: Lafayette, LA -> Memphis, TN (Driver: Sarah)
• Leg 2: Memphis, TN -> Chicago, IL (Driver: Mike)
• Passenger Selection: A drag-and-drop interface where the admin drags a dog from the "Ready to Transport" list onto the "Truck."
• CVI Verification (CRITICAL): The system should auto-check if each dog has a valid Certificate of Veterinary Inspection (Health Cert) uploaded. If a dog is missing a Health Cert, the system throws a Red Flag and won't let the manifest be finalized. This prevents the transport from getting fined by the Department of Agriculture.
Phase B: The "Digital Run Sheet" (Mobile View for Drivers)
Drivers don't need the full database; they need a simplified mobile view.
• Digital Roll Call: The driver sees a list of dogs on their truck.
• Special Instructions: Icons for "Needs Meds during trip," "Do Not Walk (Flight Risk)," or "Aggressive - Handle with Care."
• Status Updates: The driver can tap a button: "Arrived at Stop 1." This sends a notification to the Receiving Rescue so they know when to meet the truck.
Phase C: The Collaboration Hub Integration
This is where the "Network" aspect shines.
• Shared View: Both the Sending Rescue and the Receiving Rescue can view the same live Manifest.
• Chat Stream: A dedicated chat thread attached to that specific transport ID.
• Example: Receiver types: "Running 10 mins late to the meetup spot." Sender sees it instantly in the Hub.
• Chain of Custody: When the dog is handed over, the Receiver signs digitally on the Driver’s phone/tablet. This timestamped signature is saved to the Collaboration Hub as legal proof of transfer.
3. Data Fields You Need
To build this database table, you will need the following fields associated with a Transport_ID:
• Vehicle/Transport Name: (e.g., "The Northern Express - Nov 26")
• Date of Departure:
• Route: (List of Stops/Waypoints)
• Animal List: (Linked to Animal IDs)
• Documents: (Link to consolidated PDF of Health Certs + Rabies Certs)
• Emergency Contact: (Who does the driver call if the van breaks down?)
• Receiving Organization(s): (Can be multiple if the van is dropping dogs off at different rescues along the way).
