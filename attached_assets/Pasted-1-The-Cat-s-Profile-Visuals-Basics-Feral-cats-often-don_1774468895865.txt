1. The Cat's Profile (Visuals & Basics)
Feral cats often don't have names, so the visual description is the primary way trappers identify them.

Cat ID: Auto-generated by the system (e.g., TNR-2026-001).

Reference Name / Alias: (e.g., "Big Orange Tabby" or "Mama Cat").

Gender: Male, Female, or Unknown (often unknown until surgery).

Estimated Age: Adult, Kitten, or Senior.

Color / Markings: (e.g., Calico, Black, Dilute Tortie).

Photo: Highly recommended to upload a quick snapshot from the trap.

2. The Trapping & Location Data
TNR is all about geography. Rescues need to track which colonies are expanding and which are stabilized.

Colony Name / ID: (e.g., "Target Parking Lot" or "Oak Street Colony").

Exact Trapping Location: Cross streets or a specific address.

Trapper Name: Who physically set and pulled the trap.

Colony Caregiver: The name and phone number of the person who regularly feeds this colony.

Date Trapped: When the cat was pulled from the field.

3. The Medical & Surgical Event
This is the data required for almost all TNR grants (like those from PetSmart Charities or Alley Cat Allies).

Clinic / Vet Used: Which partner vet performed the spay/neuter.

Date Altered: The date of the surgery.

Ear Tip: Yes / No (This is the universal sign a feral cat has been altered. Usually the left ear).

Rabies Vaccine: Yes / No (Plus a field for the Rabies Tag # or Serial #).

FVRCP Vaccine: Yes / No.

FIV / FeLV Tested: Positive, Negative, or Not Tested.

Medical Notes: Any injuries treated, pregnancies terminated, or antibiotics given.

4. The Outcome (The Disposition)
Not every trapped cat goes back to the street. You need to track exactly where they ended up.

Outcome Type: A dropdown menu with standard options:

Returned to Colony (The standard TNR outcome)

Relocated (e.g., sent to a barn cat / working cat program)

Transferred to Rescue (If the cat turned out to be friendly/adoptable)

Euthanized in Clinic (If the cat had severe, untreatable injuries/illness)

Died in Care

Date of Outcome: When they were released or transferred.
