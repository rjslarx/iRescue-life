This is a fantastic UI/UX question. Horizontal scrolling is the absolute enemy of a good database dashboard, especially since a lot of trappers and shelter managers will be looking at this on a tablet or a smaller laptop screen.
The animal rescue and veterinary worlds rely heavily on standard shorthand. By using these industry-standard abbreviations, you can shrink your column widths by 50% without confusing your users.
Here is the cheat sheet for standard TNR and shelter abbreviations to use for your column headers:
1. Demographics & ID
 * System ID ➡️ ID
 * Reference Name/Alias ➡️ Alias * Gender ➡️ Sex (The data in the rows will just be M, F, or U)
 * Estimated Age ➡️ Age (Rows: K for Kitten, A for Adult, S for Senior)
 * Color / Markings ➡️ Color or Desc (Description)
2. Trapping Data
 * Date Trapped ➡️ Trap Date or simply Date In
 * Colony Name ➡️ Colony
 * Trapping Location ➡️ Trap Loc
 * Trapper Name ➡️ Trapper
3. Medical & Vetting (The Biggest Space Savers)
 * Date Altered ➡️ S/N Date (Spay/Neuter Date) or Sx Date (Surgery Date)
 * Ear Tipped ➡️ ET (This is universally understood in the TNR world)
 * Rabies Vaccine ➡️ RV or 1YR RV
 * FVRCP Vaccine ➡️ FVRCP (Already an abbreviation, but sometimes rescues just use Dist for Feline Distemper)
 * FIV/FeLV Status ➡️ Combo (Shelter slang for the dual-snap test. Rows will just say +, -, or NT for Not Tested)
4. Outcome
 * Outcome Type ➡️ Disp (Disposition) or Outcome
 * Date of Outcome ➡️ Out Date or Date Out
The Ultimate UI Trick: Icons + Tooltips
To make the dashboard look incredibly modern and save even more space, you can replace the header text entirely with icons for the boolean (Yes/No) medical columns.
 * Instead of "Ear Tipped", the column header is just a ✂️ icon.
 * Instead of "Rabies", the column header is a 💉 icon.
 * The Catch: You must add a Tooltip in your React code so that when a user hovers their mouse over the ✂️ icon, a little black box pops up that says "Ear Tipped".
