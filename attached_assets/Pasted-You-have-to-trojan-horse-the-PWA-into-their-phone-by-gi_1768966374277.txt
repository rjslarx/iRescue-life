You have to "trojan horse" the PWA into their phone by giving them something they need.
Here is the strategy to make this work for your tenants:
1. The "Digital Adoption Packet" Strategy
Don't ask them to download an "Alert App." Give them a "New Parent Portal."
Instead of handing them a folder of paper at adoption, the tenant sends a link: “Here is Spot’s Medical Records and Legal Ownership Transfer.”
• The Hook: To access the Vaccination Certificate (which they need for their vet/apartment), they simply "Save" the app to their phone.
• The Feature: Once installed, the PWA has a "Reminders" tab.
• Heartworm Prevention Date (Monthly)
• Rabies Expiration
• Post-Adoption Check-in
2. The Tech Stack (Don't build from scratch)
Coding raw Web Push API + Service Workers + VAPID keys is painful and fragile.
Recommendation: Use OneSignal.
• Why: They have a Free Tier that is perfect for your volume.
• The Benefit: They handle the complex "token management" (keeping track of which device belongs to "Adopter John").
• Integration: You just add their SDK to your React/Next.js frontend. When user_id: 123 (John) logs in, you call OneSignal.login('123'). Now your backend can just say "Send message to user 123," and OneSignal handles the delivery.
3. The "Hybrid" Workflow (The Safety Net)
Since push notifications can be disabled by the user, you cannot rely on them 100% for legal notices.
• Primary: Send PWA Push (Free).
• Fallback: If the user has not enabled notifications (or the push fails), fail over to Email (also Free/Cheap via SendGrid/Resend).
• Last Resort: Only use SMS if they ignore both.
4. The iOS "Gotcha" (Critical)
As of iOS 16.4, iPhones DO support Web Push, but only if the user adds the app to their Home Screen.  
• The UI Fix: You need a customized "Install Prompt" for iOS users.
• Detection: If (navigator.userAgent.match(/iPhone/)), show a floating banner with an arrow pointing to the "Share" button: "Tap Share and 'Add to Home Screen' to save your Vet Records."
5. Unique Feature Idea: "The Monthly Check-in"
To justify the notifications, make them interactive.
• Notification: "It's the 1st of the month! Have you given Spot his heartworm pill?"
• Action Buttons (in the notification): [Yes, Done] | [Remind me in 1 hour]
• Data Win: If they click "Yes," you log that in the database. The Rescue Tenant can now see a report: "90% of our adopters are compliant with meds!" — This is a huge stat for them to show their donors.
