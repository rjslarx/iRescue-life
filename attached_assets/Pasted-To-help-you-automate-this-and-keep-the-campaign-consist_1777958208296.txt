### The Replit Prompt
> **"Build a Streamlit web application that serves as a Social Media Post Generator for 'Haseya's New Beginning Animal Rescue.'**
> **The app should have the following features:**
>  1. **Input Fields:** >    - Animal Name (Text)
>    * The 'Before' (What was their situation when they arrived?)
>    * The 'After' (How are they doing now?)
>    * Monthly Subscription Link (URL)
>  2. **Logic & Formatting:**
>    * Use a 'Transformation Tuesday' theme.
>    * Automatically wrap the input into a catchy, motivational story.
>    * Include a 'Visual Cue' section at the bottom of the post using emojis to point to the link.
>    * Generate a specific 'Story Boost' instruction block for the user to follow.
>  3. **Output Display:**
>    * A text box with the final formatted post ready to copy/paste.
>    * A 'Copy to Clipboard' button.
>    * A 'Story Checklist' sidebar with reminders for the link sticker and tags.
> **Style Requirements:**
>  * The tone should be heartwarming and community-focused.
>  * Use animal-related emojis (🐾, 🐴, 🐶, ❤️).
>  * Set the background color to a soft, clean aesthetic."
> 
### What this app will do for you
Once Replit builds this, your workflow for every Tuesday will look like this:
#### 1. Input the Data
You’ll type in:
 * **Name:** *Barnaby*
 * **Before:** *Found wandering near Rayne, underweight and scared of humans.*
 * **After:** *Now a champion snuggler who just found his forever home!*
#### 2. Copy the Output
The app will instantly generate a post like this:
> 🐾 **TRANSFORMATION TUESDAY: Meet Barnaby!** 🐾
> When Barnaby first arrived at Haseya's New Beginning, he was wandering the streets of Rayne, underweight and unsure if he could ever trust a human hand.
> Today? He's a champion snuggler heading to his forever home! ❤️ These are the "Happy Tails" that your $10 makes possible.
> For the price of a couple of coffees, you can ensure the next rescue gets this same chance. Join our $10 Tuesday Club today!
> 👇 **JOIN THE HERD HERE:** > [Your Link Here]
> #HaseyasNewBeginning #10DollarTuesday #HappyTails
> 
#### 3. Follow the "Story Boost" Checklist
The sidebar in your Replit app will remind you:
 * [ ] **Post to Feed:** Use the text above with Barnaby’s photo.
 * [ ] **Share to Story:** Tap the "Paper Plane" icon on your post.
 * [ ] **Add Link Sticker:** Use the "Link" sticker in Stories and paste the subscription URL.
 * [ ] **Add Location:** Tag Rayne, LA or Lafayette, LA to increase local visibility.
### Why this works
By using Replit to build a template generator, you ensure that even on busy weeks at the rescue, the quality of the post remains high and the "Visual Cue" (pointing down to the link) is never forgotten. This structure keeps the "Happy Tail" as the emotional hook while making the "Business" side (the $10 subscription) feel like a natural part of the success story.
