Phase 2: Operational Efficiency (The "Workflow Engine")
Goal: Stop the Rescue Director from having to manually text 50 people, and prevent "I didn't have the pill" excuses.

Inventory-Aware Logic (The "Smart" Check):

The Logic: Before sending a "Give Meds" reminder, the system queries the InventoryAssignment table.

The Check: If (doses_assigned < 1), DO NOT send "Give Pill."

The Action: Instead, send: "Inventory Alert: [Dog] is due for Heartgard, but you are out. Click here to request a refill pick-up."

Protocol Templates (The Time Saver):

Create a MedicalProtocol builder.

Example: "Parvo Recovery Protocol" = 2 weeks of specific alerts.

Action: When a Director assigns "Parvo Protocol" to a dog, the system auto-generates 14 days of scheduled notifications instantly.

Bulk "Nudge" Actions:

Add a feature to the Compliance Widget allowing the Director to select multiple overdue items and send a Batch SMS: "Hi everyone, friendly reminder to update your medical logs today!"

Phase 3: The Foster Experience (Adherence & "Magic Links")
Goal: Remove all friction. No passwords, no logins, just compliance.

"Magic Link" Confirmations (No-Login Required):

The Tech: SMS contains a unique, time-sensitive token (e.g., irescue.life/action?token=xyz123).

The Action: Clicking the link opens a simple mobile web page with two big buttons:

✅ GIVEN: Updates DB to completed, logs timestamp. Shows a "Great Job!" animation (Positive Reinforcement).

⚠️ ISSUE: Opens a dropdown: "Lost Pill," "Dog Vomited," "Dog Refused." (This alerts the Medical Team immediately).

The "Download Schedule" Button (Replacing Sync):

Instead of complex syncing, provide a "Add to My Calendar" button on the Foster Dashboard.

Action: Generates a static .ics file with the next 6 months of dues dates. Simple, reliable, no API breakage.

Mandatory SMS Channels:

Hard Rule: Fosters cannot opt-out of "Critical Medical Alerts" via SMS. They can opt-out of "Event Invites" or "Newsletters," but not health safety.

Phase 4: Oversight, Liability & Funding (The "Director's View")
Goal: Give the Director the data they need to fire bad fosters and win big grants.

The "Morning Rounds" Briefing:

An automated 7:00 AM email to the Medical Director.

Content: "Good Morning. You have 3 Overdue Meds (Action Required), 1 Foster requesting a refill, and 2 Intakes pending vet checks."

Foster Reliability Scoring:

The Metric: Calculate a "Reliability Score" (0-100%) for every foster.

Calculation: (On-Time Responses / Total Requests).

Usage: If a foster drops below 70%, flag them as "High Risk" so the Director knows not to give them medically complex cases (like heartworm treatment dogs).

The "Grant Winner" Report:

A one-click CSV export designed specifically for grant applications.

Data: "In 2025, we managed 45 Heartworm Positive dogs with a 99.2% treatment compliance rate." (This is the kind of data that wins $50k grants).
