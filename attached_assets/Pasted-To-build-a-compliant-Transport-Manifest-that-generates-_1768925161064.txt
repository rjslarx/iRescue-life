To build a compliant "Transport Manifest" that generates a valid Certificate of Veterinary Inspection (CVI) (often based on USDA APHIS Form 7001), you need to capture specific data points.

If your database is missing just one of these (like the "Vaccine Lot Number"), the transport can legally be turned around at state lines.

Here are the database fields you need to add to your schema, grouped by category.

1. The "Must Have" Medical Data (The Deal Breakers)
This is where most rescues fail. You cannot just say "Rabies: Yes." You need the full metadata of the vial used.

rabies_vaccine_date_administered: (Date)

rabies_vaccine_expiration_date: (Date) – Critical for 1-year vs. 3-year logic.

rabies_vaccine_manufacturer: (String) – e.g., "Zoetis"

rabies_vaccine_product_name: (String) – e.g., "Defensor 3"

rabies_vaccine_lot_number: (String) – e.g., "345-A"

rabies_tag_number: (String) – The physical metal tag number.

microchip_number: (String) – The USDA requires "Official Identification." For dogs, this is the microchip.

2. The Animal Description
species: (String) – Dog/Cat

breed: (String) – Must be specific. "Mix" is often rejected by strict states; "Labrador Mix" is better.

age_months: (Integer) – Calculated from DOB.

sex: (String) – M/F

altered_status: (Boolean) – Spayed/Neutered?

color_and_markings: (String) – e.g., "Black with white chest."

3. The Logistics (Consignor / Consignee)
For a CVI, you must list who is shipping and who is receiving.

Consignor (Sender):

origin_name: (Your Tenant's Name)

origin_address: (Physical street address, no PO Boxes)

origin_phone: (Contact number)

Consignee (Receiver):

destination_name: (The Partner Rescue)

destination_address: (Physical address)

destination_phone: (Contact number)

4. The "Gotcha" Fields (State-Specific)
Some states (like Connecticut, Massachusetts, and Colorado) require an "Import Permit" before the truck leaves. You need a field to store this for the trip.

import_permit_number: (String) – Issued by the receiving State Vet.

inspection_date: (Date) – The CVI is usually valid for 10-30 days from this date.

accredited_vet_license_number: (String) – The license number of the vet signing the paperwork.

Developer Tip: The "Health Cert Generator"
If you want to blow your users away, build a feature that takes these fields and prints them directly onto a standard USDA APHIS Form 7001 (or a PDF replica).

The Workflow:

User clicks "Generate CVI" for a specific Transport.

System checks: "Do we have Lot Numbers for all 20 dogs?"

If Missing: It highlights the specific dog in red.

If Complete: It generates a single PDF with all animals listed (you can attach an "Addendum" page for lists of more than 5 animals).
