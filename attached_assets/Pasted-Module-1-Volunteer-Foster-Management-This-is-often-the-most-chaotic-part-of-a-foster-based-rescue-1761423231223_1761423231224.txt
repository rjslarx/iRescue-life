Module 1: Volunteer & Foster Management
This is often the most chaotic part of a foster-based rescue. These features would save hundreds of hours in back-and-forth emails and Facebook messages.

Volunteer & Transport Calendar:

What it is: A central, master calendar for the rescue. Admins and Staff can post "opportunities" or "tasks" that need to be filled (e.s., "Driver needed for vet run," "Adoption event handler," "Grant writing help").

Why it's useful: Volunteers with the "volunteer" role can log in, see the calendar, and sign up for a slot directly. It eliminates the "Who can drive on Tuesday?" emails.

Foster Portal Enhancements:

What it is: A dedicated section in the foster's dashboard for support.

Supply Request Form: A simple form where a foster can request food, medicine, crates, etc. This creates a "task" for the admin or supply coordinator.

Medical Triage Form: A simple "My foster is sick" form that asks key questions (symptoms, urgency) and immediately pings the staff/medical lead.

Module 2: Application & Adopter Workflow
This moves you from just storing data to managing a process.

Application Workflow Manager (Kanban Board):

What it is: Instead of a simple list, you see all applications for an animal on a "Trello-style" kanban board with columns like "New," "In Progress," "Vet Reference Check," "Home Visit," "Approved," "Denied."

Why it's useful: The entire team can see an application's status at a glance. You can drag-and-drop applicants, add internal notes ("Spoke to vet, all good"), and @-mention other staff members for review.

Email Template Automation:

What it is: A feature built into the Application Manager. When you move an applicant to a new column (e.g., "Approved"), you can click a button to send a pre-written email template.

Why it's useful: Saves massive amounts of time. You'd have templates for "Application Received," "Approved - Next Steps," "Denied," and "We Need Your Vet Info."

Module 3: Reporting & Finance
This is for the Board Members and Admins, helping them move from "rescuing" to "running an organization."

Automated Reporting Dashboard:

What it is: A high-level dashboard (visible to "admin" and "board_member" roles) that auto-calculates key metrics from your Firestore data.

Why it's useful: Provides instant, shareable answers to the most important questions for grants and board meetings, such as:

Total Intakes vs. Total Adoptions (This Month / YTD)

Average Length of Stay (from "Intake" status to "Adopted" status)

Total Donations vs. Total Expenditures (This Month / YTD)

Number of Active Fosters

Donor Management (Mini-CRM):

What it is: A new top-level "Contacts" collection. When someone donates or adopts, they are added to this list. The system would link all their activities.

Why it's useful: You can see a person's complete history. "Jane Doe adopted 'Buddy' in 2023 and has donated 3 times for a total of $250." This is crucial for sending targeted donation appeals and end-of-year tax receipts.

Module 4: Public Site & Fundraising
These are simple additions to the public-facing subdomain that directly increase engagement and funding.

"Sponsor a Pet" Feature:

What it is: On each adoptable animal's public page, next to the "Adopt Me" button, you add a "Sponsor Me!" button.

Why it's useful: This button links to the main donation form but pre-fills the animal's name in a "Donation in honor of..." field. This is a powerful way for people who can't adopt to give money, especially for special-needs or long-term animals.

"Happy Tails" / Alumni Gallery:

What it is: A page on the public site showcasing success stories.

Why it's useful: Builds social proof and emotional connection, which drives both adoptions and donations. You could even create a special "Alumni" role where adopters can log in and submit their own photos and updates.
