An Incredible Resource for Nonprofit Animal Rescues I cannot say enough good things about iRescue.com and the work Robby Stelly has put into creating this platform specifically for nonprofit animal rescues. Running an animal rescue means constantly juggling animals, veterinary care, applications, fundraising, community programs, paperwork, and countless other responsibilities. Time is one of the most valuable resources we have, and iRescue has created a way to make so many of those day-to-day tasks easier, faster, and more organized. The platform is extremely user-friendly and packed with useful features designed around the things animal rescues actually do. From fundraising tools and TNR tracking sheets to Happily Ever After stories, spay/neuter assistance requests, and so much more, there are tools for many different aspects of rescue work all in one place. What I especially appreciate is that the platform doesn't feel like something created by someone who doesn't understand rescue. The features are practical, effective, and genuinely useful. Instead of adding more work to an already overwhelming workload, iRescue helps simplify it. Anything that can save a rescue time means more time can be spent where it matters most — helping animals. Robby Stelly himself has also been wonderful to work with. He is friendly, professional, knowledgeable, responsive, and clearly very good at what he does. Beyond that, he is kind and compassionate, and his love for animals is evident. It makes a tremendous difference knowing that the person behind a platform created for animal welfare organizations genuinely cares about the mission and the animals these organizations are working so hard to save. For nonprofit rescues that may not have large staffs, administrative teams, or endless resources, having access to tools that help streamline operations can make an enormous difference. I would absolutely recommend iRescue to other nonprofit animal rescues looking for an easy-to-use, effective, and time-saving platform. Robby has created something that has the potential to be an incredibly valuable resource for the rescue community.
