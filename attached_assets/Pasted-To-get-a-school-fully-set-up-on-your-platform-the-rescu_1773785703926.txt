To get a school fully set up on your platform, the rescue director needs to collect a very specific, lightweight set of data. The beauty of this system is that it requires almost zero heavy lifting from the school's IT or administration.

Because we designed this with strict privacy guardrails (no student data), the school only needs to provide the high-level campaign details and the classroom roster. 

Here is the exact onboarding checklist the rescue director should send to the school's PTA president or principal:

### 1. The Campaign Basics (The "What" and "When")
* **Campaign Title:** What are we calling this? (e.g., *Lincoln Elementary's Read-a-Thon for Haseya's*)
* **The Target Goal:** Are we aiming for a specific dollar amount (e.g., $5,000) or an activity metric (e.g., 2,000 Books Read)?
* **Campaign Dates:** The exact Start Date and End Date. *(Pro tip: 2 to 3 weeks is the sweet spot. Anything longer, and parents lose a sense of urgency).*
* **The "Why" (Optional):** A short paragraph from the principal about why they chose to support this specific rescue, which will be featured on the main landing page.

### 2. The Roster (The "Who")
This is the most critical piece of information your software needs to generate those trackable sub-links. 
* **The Classroom List:** A simple spreadsheet or list of the participating classrooms. Because of child privacy laws, you do not want student names. You just need the display names for the leaderboard.
    * *Examples:* "Mrs. Smith - 3rd Grade", "Mr. Davis - 4th Grade", or "Ms. Allen's Homeroom".
* **Avatar Preferences (Optional):** If your platform allows it, the school can select which animal avatars they want to use for the classrooms, or your software can just randomly assign a mix of dogs and cats.

### 3. The Logistics (The "How")
* **Primary Point of Contact:** The name, email, and phone number of the person running the fundraiser on the school's end (usually a PTA/PTO rep). This is who the rescue will send the master link and all the classroom sub-links to once the campaign is generated.
* **School Colors/Mascot (Bonus):** If your campaign builder has a basic theme customizer, getting their primary school hex code makes the page look highly professional and builds trust with the parents.

### The SaaS Upgrade: The "Intake Form"
As the platform architect, you can turn this checklist into a massive time-saver for your rescues. Instead of having the shelter director email this list back and forth, you could eventually build a **"Campaign Intake Form"** directly into your software. 

The rescue simply clicks "Invite a School," types in the principal's email, and your software emails them a secure, branded web form to type in their dates, goal, and paste their teacher list. Once the principal hits submit, your software auto-generates the entire campaign instantly.
