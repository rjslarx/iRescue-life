The Recommended Flow: "Sign, then Pay"
Step 1: The Signature (Legal Commitment)

The adopter reads the terms (e.g., "I promise to keep the dog on a leash," "I will return the dog to the rescue if I can't keep it").

They sign the document digitally.

System Status: "Pending Payment" (The contract is signed but not yet executed).

Step 2: The Payment (Financial Commitment)

Immediately after signing, the screen redirects: "Thank you for signing. To finalize the adoption, please submit the $300 adoption fee."

They enter their credit card (via Stripe).

Step 3: Execution (Finalization)

The payment succeeds.

The system automatically countersigns the contract (or stamps it "Final").

The PDF is generated and emailed to both parties.

System Status: "Adopted" (Inventory count updates).

Why NOT "Pay First"?
If you make them pay first, you create a legal headache:

Scenario: They pay the $300. Then they read the contract and see a clause they hate (e.g., "Rescue creates right to do home checks"). They refuse to sign.

The Problem: You now have their money but no legal agreement. The rescue has to issue a refund, pay stripe fees on the refund, and deal with an angry customer.

The Rule: Always get the legal agreement before taking the money.

Why Link Them? (The "Revenue Assurance" Argument)
This is vital for your business model, especially for your Free Tier tenants.

If Unlinked: A volunteer might have the adopter sign on an iPad, then say, "Just Venmo me the $300."

Result: You (iRescue) lose your transaction fee. The rescue loses the data record.

If Linked: The volunteer says, "Fill this out on the iPad." The adopter signs and is forced to the Stripe payment screen to finish the process.

Result: You capture the fee. The database is perfect.

The "Cash Exception" (Real World Reality)
Sometimes Grandma wants to pay the $300 with a check or cash. You cannot block the adoption because she doesn't have a credit card.

The Fix: On the Admin side (Volunteer view), add a "Bypass Payment" toggle inside the contract flow.

Default: Adopter sees Credit Card form.

Admin Override: Volunteer clicks "Record Offline Payment (Cash/Check)."

Result: The contract moves to "Final" without a Stripe charge.

Summary Technical Logic
Route: /adoption/contract/:id

Action: User signs -> POST /api/sign.

Response: Server saves signature, returns { redirectUrl: '/adoption/payment/:id' }.

Action: User pays -> POST /api/charge.

Webhook: Stripe says payment_intent.succeeded.

Server: Updates Contract to status: 'active', emails PDF, marks Dog as status: 'adopted'.
