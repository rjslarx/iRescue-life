The 4 Areas for Improvement
1. The Missing "Glanceable HUD" (Bring Back the Icons!)
In the original mockup (Image 1), the magic of the card was the row of icons (✂️ Spay/Neuter, 💉 Vaccines, 🐶 Behavior).

The Issue: Right now, I can see Karma is a 43lb Pit Bull, but to figure out if she is spayed or good with kids, I have to open the card and click into her profile.

The Fix: You need to squeeze that icon row back into the collapsed state. You can place it right below the 4-quadrant grid and above the yellow location bar. That visual scan is what makes a dashboard fast.

2. Redundant "Location" Buttons
The Issue: In the expanded view (Image 3), you have a big blue "Foster" button and a "Kennel" button. But when you open the Edit dropdown (Image 4), you also have a "Change Location" button. If a staff member needs to move Karma from Row 3 to a Foster Home, they will hesitate, wondering which of the three buttons they are supposed to click.

The Fix: Consolidate. Have one single button in the expanded grid called "Move/Location" that opens a modal allowing them to assign her to a Kennel, a Foster, or the Vet.

3. The "Junk Drawer" Edit Menu
Right now, your Edit menu (Image 4) is mixing three completely different categories of actions:

Standard Edits: Edit Details, Import Vet Records.

Marketing Tools: AI Bio Generator, Generate Ad Copy.

Terminal Outcomes: Mark Deceased, Transfer, Return to Owner.

The Fix: Separate the "Outcomes" from the "Tools."

Change the generic "Edit" button to "Manage" or "Update".

Inside that dropdown, add section dividers (a faint gray line). Group the AI/Marketing tools together. Group the Terminal Statuses (RTO, Deceased, Transfer) at the very bottom in red text so people know those are "closing" actions.

4. Conflicting Status Colors (The Green Dot)
The Issue: There is a bright green dot hovering over the top left of Karma's photo. Green universally means "Available" or "Good to Go." However, her status pill in the top right explicitly says "Medical Hold" (Red), and she is HW+ (Red).

The Fix: Whatever that green dot represents (maybe it means she is currently on-site?), it is sending a mixed signal. If the dog is on Medical Hold, all visual indicators should lean red/yellow. I would consider removing the dot entirely unless it serves a critical, non-status-related function.

Suggested Action Plan for Replit
If you want to have your Replit Agent clean this up, here is what you can tell it:
