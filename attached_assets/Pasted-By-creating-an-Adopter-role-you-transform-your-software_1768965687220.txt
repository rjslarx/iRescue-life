By creating an "Adopter" role, you transform your software from a simple record-keeping tool into a "Pet Ownership Platform" that keeps the user engaged for years, not just on adoption day.
Here is how you should structure this "Adopter Portal" using your existing role-based architecture.
1. The "Adopter" Role Permissions
Unlike your Admin or Volunteer roles which see everything, the Adopter role is scoped to specific records.
• Permission Rule: Can View Animal WHERE adopter_id = current_user.id
• The UX: When they log in to the PWA, they don't see a dashboard of stats. They see "My Pets" with a big photo of their dog.
2. What belongs in their "Unique Space"?
Don't just make it a receipt. Make it a "Digital Glovebox" for their pet. This gives them a reason to keep the app installed.
• 🛡️ The "Compliance" Tab (The Hook):
• Vaccine Records: A downloadable PDF of their Rabies cert (needed for boarding/grooming).
• Microchip: The chip number and a direct link to the registry to update their address.
• 🩺 The "Health" Tab:
• Reminders: "Next Heartworm Pill: Oct 1st."
• Weight Tracker: A simple graph they can update.
• 💌 The "Alumni" Tab (The "Sticky" Feature):
• "Happy Tail" Updates: A form where they can upload a photo of the dog on the couch.
• Why this matters: Rescues are desperate for "Where are they now?" content for social media. This feeds that content directly back to the tenant.
3. The Automation Flow (How to onboard them)
The "Magic Moment" happens the second the Volunteer marks the dog as "Adopted" in your system.
1. Volunteer Action: Clicks "Finalize Adoption" and enters the Adopter's email.
2. System Action:
• Changes Dog Status to Adopted.
• Creates a User Account with role Adopter.
• Triggers Email: "Welcome Home! Here is the link to Spot's permanent medical records. Click here to access your portal."
3. No Sign-up Friction: They click the link, set a password, and boom—they are looking at their dog's records.
4. The Business Opportunity (Monetization)
Since you are building a SaaS, this "Adopter Portal" is prime real estate for ethical monetization that benefits the rescue.
• The "Donate" Button: Right next to the dog's face. "Love Spot? Buy a bag of food for his friends still at the shelter."
• Affiliate Integration: A "Shop for Spot" button.
• Link to Chewy/Amazon: "Here is the food Spot was eating at the rescue." (You or the Tenant earns the affiliate commission).
