While the tech stack (PWA, Push Notifications, Reminders Table) is identical to the Adopter Portal, the User Psychology is completely different.
• Adopters are customers. You treat them gently ("Here is your receipt").
• Fosters are staff. You treat them like employees ("Here is your job assignment").
A Foster Portal shouldn't just be a "Digital Glovebox"; it needs to be a "Co-Pilot" that manages the dog's daily operations.
Here is the modified plan for a Foster Portal.
1. The Dashboard: "The Daily Shift"
Fosters often care for sick or young animals. They don't need a "Compliance Rate"; they need a To-Do List.
The UI: Instead of a static profile, their home screen is a "Task Manager" for the day.
• ⬜ 8:00 AM: Give Amoxicillin (200mg) to Spot.
• ⬜ 12:00 PM: Weigh "Puppy A" (Weight gain is critical for neonates).
• ⬜ 5:00 PM: Upload "Golden Hour" photo for Social Media.
2. The Killer Feature: "Bio & Photo Builder" (Marketing)
The #1 bottleneck in rescues is getting good photos/bios from fosters so the dog can be posted online.
• The Feature: A dedicated tab called "Get Adopted."
• The Nag: If a dog has been in foster for >7 days and has no bio, the system sends a Push Notification: "Spot is invisible on the website! Upload a cute photo now to help him find a home."
• The Form: Don't ask for a "Bio." Ask prompts:
• Is he potty trained? [Yes/No]
• Good with cats? [Yes/No]
• Funniest quirk? [Text Box]
• Automation: Your system takes those answers and uses AI (or a template) to generate the bio automatically.
3. The "Supply Request" (Inventory Management)
Fosters shouldn't have to text the director "I'm out of food."
• The Feature: A "Request Supplies" button.
• The Menu:
• [ ] Puppy Food (Bag)
• [ ] Pee Pads
• [ ] Dewormer
• [ ] Heartworm Prevention
• The Workflow: When they click "Request," it creates a Task on the Tenant's Admin Dashboard: "Needs Preparation: Supplies for Foster Sarah."
4. The Medical Logic (Write vs. Read)
Unlike Adopters who just "View" records, Fosters need to "Write" records.
The "Medical Log" Feature:
When a Foster clicks "Done" on a "Give Meds" reminder, it shouldn't just hide the notification. It should write to the database.
• Action: Foster clicks "Given" on "Doxycycline 100mg".
• System: Inserts a row into medical_logs with user_id, timestamp, and medication.
• Why: If the dog doesn't get better, the Vet needs to know: "Did they actually miss 3 doses?" The logs provide the proof.
5. Emergency Protocol (The "Red Button")
Adopters go to their own vet. Fosters must go to the rescue's approved vet (or the rescue won't pay).
• The Feature: An "Emergency" tab.
• Content:
• "Authorized Vets": List of clinics where the rescue has an account.
• "After-Hours Protocol": "If after 6 PM, go to MedVet on Johnston St. Do NOT go to the local ER."
• "Digital Auth Card": A screen they can show the vet receptionist: "I am an authorized foster for [Rescue Name]. Bill Account #12345."
