Heartworm treatment is the most complex, expensive, and schedule-dependent medical event a rescue handles. If a volunteer misses a dosage date by a week, the dog's life is actually at risk.
This is the perfect scenario to use the "Order Sets" feature we built into your CPOE architecture. Instead of staff manually doing calendar math for 120 days of treatment, they just type "Heartworm Fast Kill Protocol," and your system automatically drops 10 perfectly timed tasks onto their calendar.
Here is the Gold Standard protocol from the American Heartworm Society (AHS) translated into software logic.
The Standard AHS "Fast Kill" Protocol
Note: Rescues sometimes use a cheaper "Slow Kill" method (just Doxycycline + monthly preventatives), but the 3-injection "Fast Kill" method below is the veterinary gold standard that requires intense scheduling.
Day 0: * Start strict exercise restriction.
Medication: Doxycycline (BID for 28 days) - Kills the symbiotic bacteria the worms need.
Preventative: Administer Heartworm Preventative (e.g., Heartgard) to kill baby worms.
Day 30:
Preventative: Administer Heartworm Preventative.
Day 60 (The First Injection):
Procedure: Melarsomine (Immiticide/Diroban) Injection #1.
Medication: Prednisone prescribed (tapering dose to reduce inflammation from dying worms).
Medication: Pain management (Gabapentin/Carprofen).
Preventative: Administer Heartworm Preventative.
Day 90 (The Second Injection):
Procedure: Melarsomine (Immiticide/Diroban) Injection #2.
Preventative: Administer Heartworm Preventative.
Day 91 (The Third Injection):
Procedure: Melarsomine (Immiticide/Diroban) Injection #3.
Medication: Prednisone prescribed (tapering dose).
Day 120:
Lab: Microfilaria test to ensure baby worms are gone.
Day 270 (6 Months Post-Treatment):
Lab: Antigen test to confirm the dog is officially Heartworm Negative.
The Architecture Upgrade: "Offset Days"
To make this work automatically, your order_sets database table needs a tiny upgrade. Right now, it just drops everything onto today's calendar. You need to add an offset_days column to the order_set_items table so the system knows when to schedule the future steps.
Here is the exact prompt to feed your Replit Agent to add these specific drugs to your catalog and upgrade your Order Sets to handle complex timeline treatments.
Copy and paste this directly into Replit Agent:
