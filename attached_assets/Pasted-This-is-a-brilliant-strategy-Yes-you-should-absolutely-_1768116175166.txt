This is a brilliant strategy.
Yes, you should absolutely give the E-Sign feature to the Free Tier, but monetize the transaction.
By allowing Free Tier tenants to use E-Sign, you create a "Revenue Trap" that benefits you. If you blocked this feature, they would just use paper contracts and take the payment via Cash/Venmo, meaning you would get $0.
Here is why this strategy works and how to structure the rules.
1. The Strategy: "The $15 Tax"
Adoption fees are usually high-value transactions (avg. $300).
 * Free Tier Rule: You can use our E-Sign tool, but the adoption fee must be processed through the app (Stripe), and we take our 5% platform fee.
   * Math: On a $300 dog, you make $15.
 * The Upgrade Trigger: The rescue quickly realizes: "Wait, we just adopted out 4 dogs. We paid iRescue $60 in fees! If we were on the $49 plan, we would have saved money."
Verdict: The feature acts as its own sales rep. The more they use it, the more "pain" they feel from the fees, which naturally pushes them to upgrade to the Paid Plan.
2. The Implementation: "Hard vs. Soft" Gating
To make this work, you need to be stricter with the Free Tier than the Paid Tier regarding "Offline Payments."
 * For Paid Tenants:
   * Contract Flow: They sign -> Payment Screen appears.
   * Option: They see a "Pay with Card" button AND a "Record Cash/Check" button.
   * Why: They pay you a subscription; you don't care if they bypass Stripe. You want to be helpful.
 * For Free Tenants (The Guardrail):
   * Contract Flow: They sign -> Payment Screen appears.
   * Option: They ONLY see the "Pay with Card" button.
   * The Trap: If they want the finalized, countersigned PDF generated by your system, the money must flow through Stripe so you get your fee.
   * Loophole Closer: If they say "Oh, the adopter has cash," they have to print a paper contract manually (losing your cool feature). This forces the digital behavior you want.
3. The "Processing Fee" Messaging
Adopters are used to paying "Ticketing Fees" (like Ticketmaster). You can pass this cost to the adopter so the rescue doesn't complain.
 * Adoption Fee: $300.00
 * Service Fee: $15.00 (Paid by Adopter)
 * Total Charge: $315.00
Why this helps: The Rescue gets their full $300. They don't mind the fee because they aren't paying it. You get your revenue. The Adopter pays for the convenience.
Summary Table
| Feature | Free Tier | Paid Tier |
|---|---|---|
| E-Sign Access | Yes | Yes |
| Adoption Fee | Mandatory via Stripe | Flexible (Stripe or Cash/Check) |
| Platform Fee | 5% (e.g., $15) | 0% |
| PDF Generation | Only after payment success | Immediate / Manual Override allowed |
| Incentive | "Pay per use" | "All inclusive" |
Recommendation
Enable E-Sign for everyone. It is the "stickiest" feature you have. Once a volunteer gets used to doing contracts on an iPad, they will never go back to paper. And once they are hooked, they will eventually upgrade to the Paid Plan to stop losing that 5% fee on every dog.
