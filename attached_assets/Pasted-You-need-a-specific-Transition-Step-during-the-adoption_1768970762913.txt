You need a specific "Transition Step" during the adoption process where the historical data is converted into a future schedule.
Here is how to build that "Review & Launch" workflow into your Tenant Dashboard.
1. The Workflow: "The Adoption Checkout"
Do not ask the rescue to set reminders manually. Build a "Release Wizard" that runs when they click "Finalize Adoption."
Step A: The Logic Check (The "Review")
The system analyzes the animal's current medical profile and presents a "Health Plan Proposal" in a modal window.
• System Logic: "I see a Rabies vax given on Oct 1, 2025. It expires in 1 year." -> Proposal: "Schedule Rabies Reminder for Sept 1, 2026?"
• System Logic: "This is a dog." -> Proposal: "Schedule Recurring Monthly Heartworm Reminder (Day 1 of Month)?"
Step B: The Human Override
The Tenant sees this list with checkboxes pre-checked.
• [x] Rabies Booster: Due Oct 1, 2026
• [x] Heartworm: Monthly (Starts Nov 1)
• [ ] Bordetella: (User can check this if needed)
• Action: The Tenant clicks "Approve & Adopt."
Step C: The Generation
The database instantly creates rows in a Reminders table.
• Why this matters: If the dog dies or is returned, you just delete these rows. You don't have to query the medical history every day to "guess" if a reminder is due.
2. The Dashboard Monitor: "Compliance, not Schedule"
The Tenant does not want to see a calendar of "500 emails sending today." That is noise.
They want to see "Who is ignoring us?"
Build a "Compliance Widget" on their main dashboard:
Widget: Adopter Health Engagement
• Metric 1: Compliance Rate (e.g., "82% of adopters confirmed heartworm meds this month").
• Metric 2: The "At-Risk" List.
• Logic: Show adopters who have missed 2 consecutive confirmations.
• Action Button: "Nudge" (Sends a manual "Is everything okay?" SMS).
• Metric 3: Expiring Soon.
• "5 dogs due for Rabies this month." (This prompts the rescue to prepare their "Use our Vet for a discount" email).
3. The Technical Schema (The "Reminders" Table)
You need a dedicated table for this. Do not try to run this off the Animals table.
Table: health_reminders
• id (PK)
• animal_id (FK)
• adopter_user_id (FK)
• type (Enum: 'Heartworm', 'Flea', 'Rabies', 'DHPP')
• due_date (DateTime)
• status (Enum: 'Pending', 'Sent', 'Confirmed', 'Missed', 'Skipped')
• recurring_interval (Enum: 'Monthly', 'Yearly', 'None')
• last_sent_at (DateTime)
4. The "Set it and Forget it" Cron Job
You run a script once a day (e.g., at 9:00 AM Local Time).
1. Find: SELECT * FROM health_reminders WHERE due_date = TODAY AND status = 'Pending'
2. Action: Fire the Push Notification / Email.
3. Update: Set status = 'Sent', last_sent_at = NOW().
4. Regenerate (If Recurring): If recurring_interval == 'Monthly', immediately create a new row for TODAY + 1 Month.
• Why create a new row? This creates a permanent history log ("They confirmed in Jan, Feb, and March, but missed April").
5. Unique Feature: The "Snooze" Logic
Real life is messy. If the reminder sends on Tuesday at 9 AM, the user might be at work.
• The Feature: In the PWA Notification, include a "Remind me Saturday" button.
• The Backend: Simply update the due_date to Saturday. The cron job will pick it up again then.
