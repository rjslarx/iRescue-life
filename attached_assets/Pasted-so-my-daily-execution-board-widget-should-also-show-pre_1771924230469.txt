The "Smart Checkbox" Logic
For a Medication (e.g., Doxycycline): The volunteer clicks the checkbox, the text gets a strikethrough, and they move on. It takes 1 second.
For a Vaccine (e.g., DHPP): The volunteer clicks the checkbox, but instead of just crossing it out, the system pops open a quick modal asking for the Manufacturer and Lot Number (the sticker peeled off the vial).
This ensures that the volunteer doesn't accidentally bypass the legal charting requirement while rushing through their morning checklist.
The Routing for Rabies (Vet Visits)
If a dog is due for Rabies, that shouldn't appear in the "In-House Meds" list. Your system should be smart enough to route that specific order down to the "Vet Visits & Surgeries" section of the widget. This tells the volunteer: "Don't look in the fridge for this one; put this dog in the van and drive to the vet."
