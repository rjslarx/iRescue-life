Close! You are right on the money regarding the Badges (Booleans), but we need to make a slight correction regarding Status vs. Location.
If you try to combine "Status" and "Location" into the same database column, your system will eventually break.
Here is the "Holy Trinity" of animal tracking in shelter software. You need to keep these as three separate concepts in your database:
1. The Status (Availability / Lifecycle)
This answers one question: "Can the public adopt this dog right now?"
This is a single dropdown field. An animal can only have ONE status at a time.
 * Available
 * Adoption Pending
 * Medical Hold
 * Stray Hold
 * Adopted
 * Transferred
2. The Location (Physical Whereabouts)
This answers the question: "Where is the dog sleeping tonight?"
This must be separate from Status. Why? Because a dog can be Status: Available but Location: Foster Home. Or, a dog can be Status: Medical Hold and also Location: Foster Home.
 * Facility / Kennel #4
 * Foster: Sarah Jenkins
 * Vet Clinic: Main St. Animal Hospital
 * In Transit: Transport TR-8294
3. The Badges / Flags (Booleans)
This is exactly what you remembered. These answer the question: "What do I need to be warned about?"
These must be Booleans (True/False checkboxes) because a dog can have many of these at the exact same time.
Medical Badges:
 * is_hw_positive (True/False)
 * needs_spay_neuter (True/False)
 * special_diet (True/False)
Behavioral Badges:
 * bite_history (True/False)
 * flight_risk (True/False) -> Escape artist
 * no_cats / no_dogs / no_kids (True/False)
Why this architecture saves you from a nightmare
Imagine a foster parent takes a dog to the vet for a 3-day stay to get Heartworm treatment.
 * If "Vet" was a Status, the dog would accidentally disappear from your website's "Available Dogs" page.
 * With the 3-Pillar system, you simply change the Location to "Vet Clinic," but leave the Status as "Available." The website stays accurate, and your staff knows exactly where the dog is.
How it maps to the Animal Card UI we designed:
 * Top Right Corner Label: Pulls from Status (e.g., a green "Available" pill).
 * Bottom Footer Row: Pulls from Location (e.g., 🏠 Foster: Sarah Jenkins).
 * Middle Icon Grid: Driven by the Badges / Flags (e.g., showing the Red Heart icon if is_hw_positive is True).
