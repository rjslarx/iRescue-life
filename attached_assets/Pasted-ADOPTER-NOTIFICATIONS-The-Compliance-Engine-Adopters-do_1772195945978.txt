ADOPTER NOTIFICATIONS (The Compliance Engine)
Adopters do not need daily pestering. Their notifications should be focused on immediate access to records, legally required compliance, and retention (making sure the adoption doesn't fail).

1. The Welcome & Digital Vault (Actionable)

Trigger: The moment the Adoption Contract is marked signed / completed.

Template: "Congratulations on adopting [Animal Name]! 🎉 Tap here to access your Adopter Portal to view your digital adoption packet, vaccine records, and microchip info: [Magic Link]"

System Action: Logs the user in without a password to view the portal we mapped out earlier.

2. The Spay/Neuter Compliance Warning (Actionable)

Trigger: 14 days before the spay_neuter_due_date stored in the adopter_compliance table.

Template: "Hi [Adopter Name]! A quick reminder from the rescue that [Animal Name]'s spay/neuter procedure is required by [Date]. Please tap here to upload a photo of your vet receipt: [Portal Link]"

System Action: If no upload happens by the due date, this triggers an Urgent Red Alert on the Shelter Manager's Daily Briefing widget.

3. The "3-3-3 Rule" Check-Ins (Informational & Retention)

Context: The 3-3-3 rule is the standard decompression timeline for rescue dogs (3 days, 3 weeks, 3 months). Reaching out automatically prevents returns.

Trigger 1 (Day 3): "Hi [Adopter Name]! It's been 3 days! [Animal Name] is likely feeling overwhelmed as they decompress. Remember to keep things quiet and positive. Need advice? Reply HELP to speak with our coordinator."

Trigger 2 (Week 3): "Happy 3 Weeks! [Animal Name] should be settling into your routine now. We'd love to see a photo of them at home! Reply with a pic!"

Trigger 3 (Month 3): "Happy 3 Month Rescue Anniversary to [Animal Name]! Thank you for giving them a forever home! 🐾"

4. The Annual Vaccine Reminder (Informational)

Trigger: 11 months after the animal's last known 1-Year Rabies or DHPP vaccine.

Template: "Hi [Adopter Name]! It's been almost a year since you adopted [Animal Name]. This is a friendly reminder that they will be due for their annual vet checkup and vaccines next month!"

The Replit Implementation Strategy
If you feed this list to your Replit Agent, you can instruct it to build a single Cron Job Service (a scheduled task that runs once every morning at 8:00 AM). The agent will write a script that scans the database, matches these exact triggers, and pushes the payloads to Twilio all at once.
