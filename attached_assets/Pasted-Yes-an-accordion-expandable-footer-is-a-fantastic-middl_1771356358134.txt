Yes, an accordion (expandable footer) is a fantastic "middle ground" solution.

It solves the problem of keeping the card small while allowing access to more data without leaving the page.

However, you have to watch out for "The Tetris Effect" (Layout Shift).

The Challenge: Grid vs. List
On Mobile (List View): Accordions are perfect. When you click "Show More," the card grows taller, and the cards below it simply slide down. It feels natural.

On Desktop (Grid View): If you have a row of 3 cards and you expand one of them, it makes that specific card taller. This creates ugly gaps in your grid or forces the entire row below to jump down.

The Solution: The "Floating" Accordion
To make this work seamlessly on both mobile and desktop, the accordion shouldn't push the other cards around. Instead, on Desktop, it should float over the content below it (using specific CSS positioning).

Here is the best way to implement the "Accordion Footer":

1. The "Chevron" Trigger
Turn the bottom footer (where it currently says "Foster: Sarah Jenkins") into a clickable button.

Visual: Add a small "Down Arrow" (Chevron) icon to the right.

Interaction: User clicks the footer row.

2. The Content Reveal
When clicked, a drawer slides down containing the "Secondary Stats" that didn't fit in the main grid:

Microchip Number

Intake Date

Exact Location / Kennel Number

Primary Vet

3. The Mobile vs. Desktop Behavior

Mobile: The card physically grows (pushes content down).

Desktop: The expanded section has a higher z-index and absolute positioning, so it "pop ups" (or drops down) over the card in the row below it, without breaking the grid alignment.

I need to upgrade the Animal Card with an "Expandable Details" accordion footer.

1. The Footer Trigger:
   - Make the bottom footer row ("Foster: Sarah Jenkins") clickable (`cursor-pointer`).
   - Add a chevron icon on the right side that rotates 180deg when open.

2. The "Hidden" Details Section:
   - Create a container for secondary stats: Microchip ID, Intake Date, Vet Name.
   - Default state: `max-h-0 opacity-0 overflow-hidden` (Hidden).
   - Open state: `max-h-40 opacity-100` (Visible).

3. The "Float" Logic (Crucial for Desktop Grid):
   - On Mobile (`< md`):
     - Just let the card grow naturally. Use `relative` positioning.
   - On Desktop (`>= md`):
     - When expanded, the detail section should effectively "float" over any content below it to prevent the grid from breaking.
     - Use `absolute top-full left-0 w-full z-50 bg-white shadow-xl rounded-b-lg border-x border-b border-gray-200`.

4. The Visual Polish:
   - Ensure the transition is smooth (`transition-all duration-300 ease-in-out`).
   - Add a slight shadow to the expanded section so it separates visually from the card below it.
Why this is a winner
Mobile Users: Get the familiar "tap to expand" experience they are used to in apps like Instagram or Amazon.

Desktop Users: Get a "clean grid" that doesn't jump around, but still gives them deep access to data without clicking away to a new page.
