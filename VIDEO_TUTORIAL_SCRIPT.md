# iRescue.life Video Tutorial - Narration Script

## Introduction (30 seconds)

"Welcome to iRescue.life, the comprehensive SaaS platform built specifically for animal rescue organizations. Whether you're a small foster-based rescue or a large shelter, iRescue.life provides everything you need to manage your operations, showcase your animals, and connect with adopters and supporters—all from a single, easy-to-use platform.

In this tutorial, we'll walk through both the public-facing website that your community sees, and the powerful staff portal that streamlines your daily operations. Let's dive in."

---

## Part 1: Public-Facing Features (8-10 minutes)

### Homepage & Branding (45 seconds)

"First, let's look at what visitors see when they come to your rescue's custom subdomain. Each organization gets their own branded site with a beautiful, mobile-responsive homepage.

The hero section prominently displays your rescue's mission and branding, with a clear call-to-action for donations and adoptions. Your custom logo, colors, and messaging create a professional first impression that builds trust with potential adopters and donors.

Notice the clean navigation menu that makes it easy for visitors to find what they're looking for—whether that's adoptable animals, upcoming events, or ways to help."

### Adoptable Animals Gallery (1 minute)

"The heart of any rescue website is the animals available for adoption. On the Animals page, visitors can browse through all your adoptable pets with beautiful photo cards.

Each card shows the animal's photo, name, species, breed, age, and current status. Visitors can filter by species—dogs, cats, rabbits, or other animals—and search by name to quickly find specific pets.

Let's click on one of these animals to see their full profile. The detailed animal page includes multiple photos, a complete description, personality traits, medical information, and adoption requirements. This gives potential adopters everything they need to know before applying.

Right from this page, interested adopters can click the 'Apply to Adopt' button to start the application process."

### Adoption Application Process (1 minute)

"The adoption application system is fully customizable. Your organization can create custom form fields that capture exactly the information you need from applicants.

As you can see, the form includes standard fields like name, email, and phone number, plus custom questions about living situation, experience with animals, veterinary references, and more. The form is clean, user-friendly, and works perfectly on mobile devices.

Once submitted, applicants receive an automatic confirmation email, and your staff is notified immediately. We'll show you how staff members manage these applications in the backend portion of this demo."

### Donation System (1 minute)

"Supporting your rescue financially is simple with the integrated Stripe payment processing. The donation page allows supporters to choose a donation amount or enter a custom amount.

Donors can make one-time donations or set up recurring monthly support. The checkout process is secure, PCI-compliant, and accepts all major credit cards.

After completing their donation, supporters receive an automated thank-you email with their receipt. All donation data automatically flows into your financial reports in the staff portal."

### Events Calendar (45 seconds)

"The public events calendar showcases all your upcoming adoption events, fundraisers, and community activities. Each event displays the date, time, location, and description.

Visitors can click on individual events to see full details, including how to RSVP or get directions. Events automatically sync with Google Calendar if you've connected your Google Workspace for Nonprofits account, and can even include Google Meet links for virtual events."

### Happy Tails - Success Stories (30 seconds)

"One of the most powerful ways to inspire donations and applications is through success stories. The Happy Tails section showcases your adopted animals living their best lives in their forever homes.

Each story includes before-and-after photos, the adoption date, and a heartwarming description of the animal's journey. These stories build emotional connections and demonstrate the impact of your rescue's work."

### Supply Wishlist (45 seconds)

"The Supply Wishlist makes it easy for supporters to help in practical ways. Visitors can see exactly what items your rescue needs—from dog food and cat litter to cleaning supplies and medical equipment.

Each item shows the name, quantity needed, and direct links to purchase from retailers like Amazon, Chewy, or Walmart. Supporters can click through and have items shipped directly to your rescue. Your staff can update this list in real-time as needs change."

### Volunteer Opportunities (1 minute)

"Animal rescues run on volunteer power, and the Volunteer section makes recruitment easy. The page displays all available volunteer opportunities with descriptions, time commitments, and requirements.

Interested volunteers can click 'Sign Up' to complete an application right on your website. The volunteer application form is customizable, allowing you to collect background information, availability, skills, and preferences.

For volunteers who are already part of your team, they can view and sign up for specific shifts directly from the calendar—we'll show you how staff manages this in the backend."

### Foster Program (45 seconds)

"Foster families are critical to rescue operations. Your public Foster page explains your foster program, requirements, and the application process.

Interested foster families complete a detailed application that captures household information, experience, and preferences for the types of animals they can care for. Once approved, foster families gain access to special features where they can submit supply requests and medical updates—keeping your staff informed in real-time."

### Animal Surrender Requests (30 seconds)

"Sometimes people need to surrender their pets to your rescue. The surrender request form allows them to submit detailed information about the animal, including photos, medical history, and the reason for surrender.

Staff members receive these requests in their portal where they can review, approve, or decline based on capacity and fit."

### Newsletter Subscription (15 seconds)

"Visitors can subscribe to your newsletter right from the homepage footer. Email addresses are automatically added to your mailing list, and your staff can send targeted email campaigns to keep supporters engaged."

### Custom Content Pages (30 seconds)

"Beyond the standard pages, you can create unlimited custom content pages for things like your rescue's story, adoption policies, FAQ, or special campaigns. Each page supports rich text, images, and can be published or unpublished as needed."

## Part 2: Staff Portal & Internal Features (15-18 minutes)

### Introduction to Staff Portal (30 seconds)

"Now let's switch to the staff portal—the command center for your rescue operations. Staff members log in with their email and password, and the system supports role-based access control, so you can control what each team member can see and do.

The platform supports multiple roles including administrators, staff members, volunteers, and foster families. You can even switch between roles if you have multiple permissions."

### Dashboard Overview (1 minute)

"After logging in, staff members land on the comprehensive dashboard. This is your at-a-glance view of everything happening in your rescue.

At the top, you'll see key metrics: total animals in care, animals available for adoption, pending applications, active fosters, and monthly donation totals.

Below that, you'll find quick action buttons to add new animals, create events, or access frequently-used features.

The recent activity feed shows real-time updates—new applications, donations, foster updates, and system events. The urgent items section highlights things that need immediate attention, like applications awaiting review or animals overdue for medical care.

The upcoming events calendar helps your team stay coordinated, and the trend indicators show whether key metrics are moving up or down compared to previous periods."

### Animal Management System (2 minutes)

"Let's explore the comprehensive animal management system. The Animals page displays all animals currently in your care with their photo, name, species, breed, age, status, and current location.

You can filter by species, status, or kennel location, and search by name or microchip number. The status options include Available for Adoption, In Foster, Adopted, On Hold, Medical Hold, and more.

Let's add a new animal to the system. Click the 'Add Animal' button, and you'll see the detailed intake form. Enter the animal's name, species, breed, age, sex, size, color, and microchip number.

You can upload multiple photos—the first one becomes the primary photo shown in galleries. Add a detailed description that highlights personality, behavior, and special needs.

Set the adoption fee, intake date, and initial status. If the animal is going to a foster home, you can assign them immediately. You can also specify their kennel location for shelter-based animals.

Once saved, this animal appears in your system and—if marked as available for adoption—automatically shows up on your public website."

### Kennel Management System (1.5 minutes)

"For shelter-based rescues, the kennel management system is invaluable. Navigate to the Kennels page to see your visual kennel layout.

You can create individual kennels or use the bulk creation tool to set up entire rows at once. For example, enter 'A' as the row and '1-10' as the range, and the system creates kennels A1 through A10 automatically.

Each kennel card shows its location, current occupancy status, capacity, and which animal is assigned. The color coding makes it easy to see at a glance which kennels are occupied (blue), which are empty (gray), and which are at capacity.

You can click on a kennel to see details, edit capacity, or reassign animals. The drag-and-drop interface makes it simple to move animals between kennels.

This visual system helps staff quickly locate animals, manage capacity, and maintain organized shelter operations."

### Adoption Application Workflow (2 minutes)

"Managing adoption applications is one of the most important—and time-consuming—parts of rescue work. iRescue.life streamlines this with a powerful Kanban-style workflow board.

Applications move through stages: New, Under Review, Home Visit Scheduled, Approved, Denied, and Adopted. Each card shows the applicant's name, the animal they're applying for, submission date, and status.

Let's click on an application to see the details. You can review all the information the applicant submitted—their contact information, household details, experience with animals, veterinary references, and answers to your custom questions.

From here, staff can update the application status, add internal notes that only staff can see, and communicate with the applicant. When you change the status, the applicant receives an automatic email notification.

The archive feature automatically moves applications that are denied or adopted to an archived state after 7 days, keeping your active queue focused on applications in progress.

This workflow ensures no application falls through the cracks, and multiple team members can collaborate on the same application."

### Foster Management (1.5 minutes)

"The foster management system helps you coordinate your foster network. The Fosters page shows all active foster families, the animals in their care, and their contact information.

When you assign an animal to foster care, you can select the foster family, set the placement date, and add any special instructions or notes.

Foster families have their own portal access where they can submit supply requests and health updates. Let's look at the Foster Communications page where these submissions appear.

Supply requests show what items the foster family needs, the urgency level, and the animal they're caring for. Staff can mark these as fulfilled or archived once handled.

Foster updates allow foster families to report on the animal's health, behavior, and any concerns. This keeps your team informed without requiring constant phone calls or texts.

Medical concerns are flagged with high priority so staff can respond immediately to health issues. All communications are timestamped and archived for record-keeping."

### Medical Records System (2 minutes)

"Comprehensive medical record-keeping is critical for rescue operations. Click on any animal and navigate to their Medical Records tab.

Here you can track all medical care across multiple categories:

**Vaccinations**: Record vaccine type, date administered, due date for boosters, veterinarian, and notes. The system can flag when vaccines are coming due.

**Examinations**: Document routine checkups, intake exams, and health assessments with findings, veterinarian, and date.

**Diagnostics**: Track lab work, x-rays, blood tests, and other diagnostic procedures with results and files.

**Procedures**: Log spay/neuter surgeries, dental cleanings, wound repairs, and other medical procedures.

**Prescriptions**: Manage ongoing medications with dosage, frequency, prescribing veterinarian, start and end dates.

**Medical Bills**: Track all veterinary expenses associated with each animal, linking costs to specific procedures or treatments.

Everything is organized chronologically, giving you a complete medical history at a glance. This is invaluable during vet visits, for home checks, and when discussing an animal's needs with potential adopters.

You can also export medical records to PDF for sharing with adopters or other veterinarians."

### Financial Management (1.5 minutes)

"The Finance section gives you complete visibility into your rescue's financial health. The dashboard shows total donations received, total expenditures, and net income for the current month and year.

The Donations table lists every contribution—whether made online through your website, entered manually for cash/check donations, or recurring subscriptions. You can see donor names, amounts, dates, and payment methods.

Click 'Add Donation' to manually record offline donations—perfect for checks received in the mail or cash donations at events.

The Expenditures section tracks all your rescue's expenses. Record purchases of supplies, veterinary bills, facility costs, and administrative expenses. Each entry includes date, amount, category, description, and optional receipt upload.

The financial reports feature generates visual charts showing donation trends over time, expense breakdowns by category, and monthly comparisons. Export these reports to Excel for accounting purposes or board meetings.

This financial transparency is crucial for tax reporting, grant applications, and demonstrating fiscal responsibility to donors."

### Contact Management (45 seconds)

"The Contacts page is your centralized directory of everyone who's interacted with your rescue. The system automatically creates contact records when people submit applications, make donations, or sign up as volunteers.

Each contact record includes their full name, email, phone, address, and a timeline of all their interactions with your rescue—donations made, applications submitted, volunteer shifts completed.

You can manually add contacts, update information, and add notes. This makes it easy to build relationships with your supporters and recognize repeat donors or dedicated volunteers."

### Event Management with Calendar (1.5 minutes)

"The Events and Calendar system helps you coordinate your rescue's schedule. Switch to calendar view to see all upcoming events in a monthly, weekly, or daily layout.

Create new events by clicking on any date. Enter the event title, description, start and end times, location, and whether it should be visible on your public website.

Here's where the Google Workspace integration really shines: If you've connected your Google Workspace for Nonprofits account, events automatically sync to your Google Calendar. Your entire team sees updates in real-time.

Even better, when creating virtual events like remote home visits or volunteer orientations, you can check the 'Add Google Meet link' box. The system automatically generates a unique Google Meet video conference URL and includes it in the event details. Staff and participants receive the Meet link in their calendar invites—no manual setup required.

You can link events to custom content pages on your website, allowing visitors to click through for more detailed information about fundraisers or special campaigns.

The calendar supports multiple event types: adoption events, fundraisers, vet appointments, volunteer shifts, and more. Color-coding helps staff distinguish between event types at a glance."

### Volunteer Coordination (1 minute)

"Managing volunteers is simplified with the Volunteer Coordination system. The Opportunities page shows all available volunteer positions with descriptions, requirements, and time commitments.

Create new opportunities for things like dog walking, cat socialization, transport, event help, or administrative support. Set the number of volunteers needed and the schedule.

When volunteers sign up through your public website, their applications appear here for review. You can approve or decline volunteers, and approved volunteers can then sign up for specific shifts.

The volunteer calendar shows who's scheduled for what shifts. You can assign volunteers to opportunities, track hours contributed, and send automated reminder emails before shifts.

This system ensures you always have adequate volunteer coverage and makes volunteers feel valued and organized."

### Happy Tails Management (30 seconds)

"Create heartwarming success stories in the Happy Tails section. Upload before-and-after photos of adopted animals, write their story, and set the adoption date.

Published stories automatically appear on your public website, inspiring visitors and demonstrating the impact of your rescue work. You can unpublish or archive old stories as needed."

### Supply Wishlist Management (30 seconds)

"Update your public supply wishlist from the Supply Registry page. Add items your rescue needs, set quantities, select categories, and add links to Amazon, Chewy, or Walmart where supporters can purchase items.

Mark items as fulfilled when received, or remove them when no longer needed. This real-time updating keeps your supporters informed about your most pressing needs."

### Email Campaigns & Newsletters (1 minute)

"The Email Campaigns feature allows you to communicate with your supporters at scale. Create newsletters to update your community about adoptions, events, and needs.

Compose your message using the rich text editor, add images, and preview how it will look in recipients' inboxes.

If you've connected Google Workspace for Nonprofits, emails send through Gmail's API—giving you free unlimited sending within Google's limits. Without Google Workspace, emails send through our integrated Resend service with tier-based quotas.

Target specific segments: send to all newsletter subscribers, donors, volunteers, or foster families. The system tracks who received each campaign and when.

Beyond campaigns, the system sends automatic transactional emails for application confirmations, donation receipts, password resets, and status updates—keeping everyone informed without manual effort."

### Document Management (45 seconds)

"Store and organize important documents in the Document Library. Upload adoption contracts, volunteer agreements, medical records, insurance certificates, and any other files your rescue needs to maintain.

Organize documents by category and control access with role-based permissions—some documents might be visible only to administrators, while others can be accessed by all staff.

With Google Drive integration, you can connect your rescue's Google Workspace account and access files directly from within iRescue.life, combining cloud storage with your operational workflow."

### User & Team Management (1 minute)

"Manage your team members in the Users section. Add new staff members, volunteers, or foster families by sending email invitations. Invitees receive a secure link to set up their password and join your organization.

Assign roles that determine what each user can access and do: Administrators have full control, Staff can manage animals and applications, Volunteers have limited access to their shifts and information, and Foster Families can only see their assigned animals and submit updates.

Enable or disable user accounts as team members join or leave. The system maintains an audit trail of who made changes and when—important for security and accountability.

For platform administrators, there's even a special 'Platform Admin' role for the owners of iRescue.life who can manage multiple rescue organizations across the entire platform."

### Branding & Site Settings (1 minute)

"Customize your rescue's branding in the Settings section. Upload your logo, set your primary brand color, and write your mission statement.

Configure your rescue's contact information, address, phone number, and social media links—all of which appear on your public website.

The settings include email notification preferences, adoption fee defaults, and operational preferences.

You can also manage your custom domain setup if you want to use your own domain name (like www.yourrescue.org) instead of the provided subdomain."

### Content Management System (45 seconds)

"The Content Pages section is your CMS for creating custom pages on your website. Create pages for your rescue's history, adoption policies, FAQs, donor recognition, or special campaigns.

Use the rich text editor to format content, add images, embed videos, and create compelling pages that tell your rescue's story.

Set page-level permissions to control which roles can view each page—useful for creating private pages accessible only to logged-in volunteers or foster families.

Publish or unpublish pages as needed. Published pages automatically appear in your website's navigation menu."

### Adoption Form Customization (45 seconds)

"Every rescue has different adoption requirements, so the adoption form is fully customizable. In the Form Settings section, add, edit, or remove form fields.

Create text fields for short answers, text areas for longer responses, checkboxes for yes/no questions, dropdowns for multiple choice, and number fields for numerical data.

Mark fields as required or optional. Reorder fields by dragging and dropping.

The same customization capabilities exist for volunteer applications and foster applications—allowing you to collect exactly the information your rescue needs for each program."

### Google Workspace Integration (1.5 minutes)

"For rescue organizations with Google Workspace for Nonprofits, iRescue.life offers seamless integration with Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, and Google Meet.

Navigate to the Integrations page and click 'Connect Google Workspace.' You'll authenticate with your Google account, granting iRescue.life permission to access your Gmail, Calendar, and Drive on behalf of your rescue.

Once connected, several powerful features activate:

**Gmail Integration**: All emails sent by the system—application confirmations, donation receipts, newsletters—route through Gmail's API instead of our standard email service. This gives you unlimited free email sending (within Google's generous limits) and emails appear to come from your professional @yourrescue.org email address.

**Calendar Sync**: Events created in iRescue.life automatically appear in your Google Calendar, and your team can see them alongside their personal schedules. Automatic reminders from Google Calendar ensure nothing gets missed.

**Google Meet**: When creating virtual events, the system generates Google Meet video conference links automatically and includes them in calendar invites—perfect for remote home visits, virtual adoption meet-and-greets, or online volunteer training sessions.

**Drive Storage**: Access your Google Drive files directly from within iRescue.life, attach documents to animal profiles or adoption applications, and leverage your 100TB of free nonprofit storage.

This integration transforms iRescue.life from a standalone platform into a hub that connects all your existing Google tools—eliminating the need to switch between multiple browser tabs."

"The Reports section provides insights into your rescue's performance. View trends in adoptions over time, donation patterns, application conversion rates, and volunteer engagement.

Visual charts and graphs make it easy to see what's working and where you might need to adjust your strategies.

Export reports to Excel for deeper analysis or presentation to your board of directors. This data-driven approach helps you make informed decisions about resource allocation and program development."

### Tips for Recording:
1. **Screen Recording**: Capture both mouse movements and clicks to help viewers follow along
2. **Pacing**: Speak clearly and pause briefly between sections to let information sink in
3. **Demo Data**: Use realistic sample data (animal names, descriptions, etc.) rather than placeholder text
4. **Transitions**: Use simple fade or dissolve transitions between major sections
5. **Call-to-Action**: End with website URL and contact information displayed on screen
6. **Captions**: Consider adding captions for accessibility
7. **Background Music**: Use subtle background music at low volume, fading during detailed explanations
8. **Visual Highlights**: Use zoom-ins or highlights to draw attention to specific UI elements when explaining features

### Suggested Video Structure:
- **Introduction**: 0:00 - 0:30
- **Part 1 (Public Features)**: 0:30 - 10:30
- **Part 2 (Staff Portal)**: 10:30 - 28:30
- **Conclusion & CTA**: 28:30 - 30:00
