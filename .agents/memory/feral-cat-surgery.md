Haseya’s applicants requesting surgery for feral cats must not need an organization affiliation, but must provide an email address.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this exception for Haseya’s and required email for feral-cat applicants, not a platform-wide change to other surgery requests.

**How to apply:** Preserve this individual-applicant route when revising surgery-day intake; retain contact information and consent requirements.
