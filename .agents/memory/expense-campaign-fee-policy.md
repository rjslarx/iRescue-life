---
name: Expense campaign fee policy
description: Donor opt-in and rescue responsibility for expense fundraiser fees.
---

Expense campaign donors may voluntarily contribute toward processing fees. Without that opt-in, fees must come from the rescue's proceeds, not be added to the donor's charge or absorbed by iRescue.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed this allocation on 2026-11-07. These campaigns use the rescue's Standard Connect direct-charge flow; avoid converting them to platform charges casually.

**How to apply:** Preserve direct-charge ownership. Keep fee contributions separate from the gift for campaign progress and angel matching. Label calculated card-fee coverage as an estimate rather than claiming it equals Stripe's actual settled fee.
