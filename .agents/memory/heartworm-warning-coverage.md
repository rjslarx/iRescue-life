---
name: Heartworm warning coverage
description: Enforcement notices must evaluate heartworm coverage separately from product history and flea/tick care.
---

Treat heartworm warnings as coverage-specific, not brand-specific. Never clear an old combination-product date merely because a newer heartworm-only dose exists: it may still represent an unmet flea/tick requirement.

**Why:** An adopter received an overdue warning from an old combination-product record despite a current six-month heartworm injection. Whole-record supersession cannot solve partial coverage switches safely. The user requested prevention of this problem across all tenants.

**How to apply:** For heartworm notices use the newest administered, non-future heartworm-covered record. Pending doses are not evidence of coverage. Keep historical dates and sent-warning audit records intact. Verify catalog coverage flags rather than assuming all parasite prevention covers heartworm.
